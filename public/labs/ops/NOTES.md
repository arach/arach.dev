# Illustration plates

Wrote `wash.png`, `hero-plate.png`, `keys.png`, and `grain.png`. The set uses a restrained matte copperplate/print-shop treatment on warm policy-paper stock: the wash stays airy and low-contrast, the hero seal pairs oxidized copper rings with a teal breath curve, the keys are cropped into a dusty lamp-lit engraving, and the grain is a subtle teal-flecked paper texture suitable for card overlays. All plates avoid words, logos, and faces.
