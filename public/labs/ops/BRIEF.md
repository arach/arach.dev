# Ops lab illustrations

Generate four atmospheric plates for `/labs/ops` on arach.dev. Save them in this folder with the exact filenames below. Do not write application code. Do not put words, letters, numbers, logos, or watermarks in any image.

This is not a resume. Do not depict companies, org charts, city skylines, or a person's face. The motif is **breath + keys**: a life-insurance policy paper world (Breathe Life) meeting a warm desk lamp and a keyboard after a long time in meeting rooms.

## Palette (lock this)

- Paper: `#efe9dc`
- Ink: `#1c241f`
- Breath teal: `#2f6a62`
- Key copper: `#c4622d`
- Dust: `#5e6a63`

Print-shop / copperplate / oxidized metal. Matte. No neon. No UI chrome. No 3D product shots.

## Files

1. `wash.png` — 2400×1350. A wide, almost-empty field of policy paper. One faint breath-waveform or spirometer curve drifting through. Soft copper oxidation at the edges. Must sit behind type at ~20% opacity, so keep contrast low.

2. `hero-plate.png` — 1400×1400. A circular plate / seal: concentric rings, a single inhale-exhale curve through the middle, copper on paper. No text. Will sit beside the headline like a stamp.

3. `keys.png` — 1800×1000. Extreme close crop of typewriter slugs or laptop keycaps in warm lamp light, abstracted toward etching. Dusty, quiet. No readable letters if possible (blank or worn slugs).

4. `grain.png` — 1600×1600 tileable-feeling paper grain with a whisper of teal oxidation. Subtle enough to overlay on cards.

If you cannot generate images in this environment, write the four prompts you would use into `PROMPTS.md` and stop. If you can generate, write the files and a one-paragraph note in `NOTES.md` describing the look you chose.
